About

Perry Machinery Czech Republic, s.r.o.

Authorised Hosokawa Micron B.V. partner for the Czech and Slovak Republics. We deliver new industrial machinery for mixing, milling and drying.

What we do

We specialise in the supply and service of Hosokawa Micron industrial machinery - a world leader in technology for powder and liquid materials. Our customers are companies in the chemical, pharmaceutical, food and cosmetics industries.

Why Hosokawa Micron

Hosokawa Micron B.V. is a Dutch company with over 100 years of experience. Its machines define the standards for hygiene, precision and energy efficiency. Our role: a local partner with full OEM support and a service base in Hradec Králové.

What we supply

Vertical homogenizers · ACM milling systems · spray dryers · mixing reactors · dust filtration. All in original Hosokawa configuration with full manufacturer warranty, technical documentation and operator training.

Service and support

Local CZ/SK service team with a 48-hour response time. Spare-parts warehouse in Hradec Králové. Preventive maintenance, calibration, validation and upgrades under long-term service contracts.
