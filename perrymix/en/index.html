Applications

Where our homogenizers prove themselves

What the HV vertical homogenizer does for your industry. Click an industry to expand the detail.

Food Puddings, soups, instant drinks, tea and spice blends. Show detail

In food production the HV homogenizer evenly blends powder and bulk ingredients so every batch has the same composition, colour and taste. Gentle conical-screw mixing does not damage sensitive ingredients and prevents fine fractions from separating. Supplied in a food-grade build - product-contact parts in AISI 304 stainless steel (AISI 316L on request), smoothly ground welds and easy cleaning between batches.

  • Puddings, desserts and instant blends
  • Soups, sauces and dehydrated meals
  • Tea, spice and seasoning blends
  • Instant and beverage powders
Pharma Blends for medicines and food supplements. Show detail

For pharmaceutical production the homogenizer delivers a high, repeatable degree of homogenization - decisive for the uniform distribution of the active ingredient across the whole batch. The self-discharging effect and quick screw removal ease cleaning and reduce the risk of cross-contamination between batches. Product-contact parts are supplied in AISI 316L.

  • Blends for medicine manufacturing
  • Food supplements and vitamin premixes
  • Powder blends of active and excipient substances
Chemicals Powder paints, pigments and chemical blends. Show detail

In chemical production the machine evenly blends powders, pigments and bulk chemical mixtures even with differing particle size and density. For explosive atmospheres we supply an ATEX-rated drive and gearbox and a pneumatically controlled discharge valve.

  • Powder paints and coatings
  • Pigments and fillers
  • Dry chemical blends and compositions
Feed and agrochemistry Feed blends and agricultural premixes. Show detail

In feed and agrochemical production the homogenizer quickly and evenly blends small shares of additives (vitamins, minerals, medication) into a large volume of carrier mix. A high degree of homogenization and short mixing time ensure the active component is evenly distributed in every kilogram of product.

  • Feed blends and supplements
  • Agricultural and veterinary premixes
  • Micro-components (vitamins, minerals) into carrier mixes

FAQ

What customers ask most often

The most common questions from procurement, R&D and QA before starting an enquiry. Didn't find your answer? Write to us.

What HV homogenizer sizes do you manufacture?

The HV range covers 10 model sizes from HV50 (50 L) to HV3000 (3,000 L). We fine-tune the exact size and design to your application and batch volume.

What materials is the vertical homogenizer suitable for?

The machine is a conical screw mixer - an orbiting arm with a rotating mixing screw in a conical vessel. It suits powder and bulk blends in particular: food, spices, medicines, feed premixes, powder paints and pigments.

What is the material design?

Standard design: vessel, screw, traverse and covers in AISI 304 stainless steel; arm and drive in carbon steel with food-grade coating. On request we supply a fully stainless arm and product-contact parts in AISI 316L.

Can the machine be supplied for explosive atmospheres (ATEX)?

Yes. Optionally we supply a drive and gearbox for explosive atmospheres, a pneumatically operated discharge valve and other adjustments to your requirements.

Can the machine be financed by leasing or an investment loan?

Yes. We work with several finance partners (operating lease, finance lease, investment loan). Specific terms are arranged together with the price quote.

Do you provide service and spare parts?

Yes. We are the manufacturer, with a service base in Hradec Kralove. We supply spare parts as the manufacturer, with long-term availability as a matter of course.
