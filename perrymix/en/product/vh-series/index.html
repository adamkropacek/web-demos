Basic design

  • Vessel, mixing screw, clamping traverse, outlet and semi-circular hoods in AISI 304 stainless steel.
  • Orbit arm, drive and gearbox in carbon steel sprayed with food-grade paint.
  • Vessel parts in contact with the product, including welds, are ground smooth.
  • Vessel wall thickness before processing is 2.5 mm.

Custom design (on request)

  • All-stainless arm in grade AISI 304.
  • Product-contact parts in grade AISI 316L.
  • Quick-removal screw for faster, easier assembly and disassembly.
  • Tilting half-circle top cover with locking.
  • Drives and gearboxes for potentially explosive atmospheres (ATEX).
  • Pneumatically controlled discharge valve.

Mixing principle

Fast and effective homogenization takes place by moving the arm and the stirring screw. The homogenized raw materials are drawn from the bottom of the vessel upwards by screw rotation. At the same time the screw rotates along the inner conical wall of the vessel and moves and mixes the product. The product then slides under gravity back to the bottom of the vessel.
