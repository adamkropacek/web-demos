Treatments

Injectable and skin treatments, chosen together at a free consultation. Choose a treatment to see what it does, how long it takes and when you will see the result.

  • Botox

    Small doses of botulinum toxin relax the muscles that fold the skin, so forehead lines, frown lines and crow's feet soften without a frozen look. Also used for a brow lift, chronic migraine and excessive sweating.

    Session
    20 to 30 minutes
    Downtime
    None
    First results
    3 to 5 days, full effect at 2 weeks
    Lasts
    3 to 6 months

    Good for: expression lines, a heavy brow, a gummy smile, jaw clenching, sweating.

    Ask about Botox

  • Dermal fillers

    Hyaluronic-acid fillers restore volume and definition: lips, cheeks, chin, jawline, nasolabial folds, tear troughs and non-surgical nose correction. We work with needle or cannula, whichever is safer for the area.

    Session
    30 to 45 minutes
    Downtime
    Mild swelling for 1 to 3 days
    First results
    Immediately, settled after 2 weeks
    Lasts
    6 to 18 months, depending on the area

    Good for: thin lips, flat cheeks, a weak chin or jawline, a nose bump, hollows under the eyes.

    Ask about fillers

  • PRP therapy

    Platelet-rich plasma from a small sample of your own blood, prepared in the clinic and injected where you need it: into the face to stimulate collagen and improve texture, or into the scalp to strengthen thinning hair.

    Session
    45 to 60 minutes
    Downtime
    None, slight redness for a day
    First results
    A few weeks
    Course
    Usually a short series of sessions

    Good for: dull or tired skin, acne scars, fine lines, hair thinning.

    Ask about PRP

  • Mesotherapy

    Micro-injections of vitamins, amino acids and hyaluronic acid placed just under the skin or into the scalp. Brightens tired skin, improves hydration and supports hair growth.

    Session
    30 to 40 minutes
    Downtime
    None
    First results
    After 2 to 3 sessions
    Course
    4 to 6 sessions, then maintenance

    Good for: dehydrated skin, loss of glow, early hair loss, dark circles.

    Ask about mesotherapy

  • Biorevitalisation

    Pure hyaluronic acid placed in the skin to rehydrate it from within. Over the following weeks fine lines soften, tone evens out and the skin looks rested.

    Session
    About 30 minutes
    Downtime
    Small bumps at injection points for 1 to 2 days
    First results
    1 to 2 weeks
    Course
    2 to 3 sessions

    Good for: dry, dull or sun-damaged skin, first fine lines, before an important event.

    Ask about biorevitalisation

  • Skin boosters

    A blend of vitamins, minerals and hyaluronic acid that stimulates collagen and elastin. For the face, neck, hands and décolletage: firmer, smoother and better-hydrated skin.

    Session
    About 30 minutes
    Downtime
    None
    First results
    2 to 4 weeks
    Lasts
    Around 6 months

    Good for: crepey neck and hands, loss of firmness, skin that looks tired despite good skincare.

    Ask about skin boosters

  • Facial cleansing

    A deep cleansing and hydration facial that clears congestion, prepares the skin for other treatments and restores glow. A good first visit if you are not ready for injectables.

    Session
    45 to 60 minutes
    Downtime
    None
    First results
    Immediately
    Repeat
    Every 4 to 6 weeks

    Good for: congested or oily skin, a dull complexion, preparation before an event.

    Ask about facial cleansing

Times and durations are typical. Your plan, the number of sessions and the price are agreed at the consultation, before any treatment.

Questions patients ask

Is it safe?

Botox and hyaluronic-acid fillers are among the most studied procedures in aesthetic medicine and are safe when a trained physician uses original products. Both of our doctors are licensed physicians, and you can see the product documentation at your visit.

Does it hurt?

Most people feel only minimal discomfort. We use numbing cream, fine needles or a cannula, and the injections themselves take a few minutes.

When will I see the result?

Fillers show immediately and settle within two weeks. Botox starts working in 3 to 5 days, with the full effect at two weeks. PRP, mesotherapy and boosters build up over several weeks as the skin responds.

Is there downtime?

Usually none. Most people go straight back to their day. Fillers can leave mild swelling or a small bruise for a few days, which is easy to cover.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the area and the amount of product. You get the exact price at the free consultation, before any treatment, with no obligation.

I am travelling to Tbilisi. Can I do everything in one visit?

Many of our patients fly in from Israel and Europe. Send us your dates and what you have in mind on WhatsApp, and we will plan the consultation, the treatment and the follow-up check around your stay.
