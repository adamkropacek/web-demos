Four steps, no surprises

  1. Free consultation

    In the clinic, by phone or by video call. We look at your face, listen to what bothers you, and tell you honestly what will help and what you do not need.

Questions

What patients ask before booking

Is it safe?

Botox and hyaluronic-acid fillers are among the most studied procedures in aesthetic medicine and are safe when a trained physician uses original products. Both of our doctors are licensed physicians, and you can see the product documentation at your visit.

Does it hurt?

Most people feel only minimal discomfort. We use numbing cream, fine needles or a cannula, and the injections themselves take a few minutes.

When will I see the result?

Fillers show immediately and settle within two weeks. Botox starts working in 3 to 5 days, with the full effect at two weeks. PRP, mesotherapy and boosters build up over several weeks.

Is there downtime?

Usually none. Fillers can leave mild swelling or a small bruise for a few days, which is easy to cover.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the area and the amount of product. You get the exact price at the free consultation, before any treatment, with no obligation.

I am travelling to Tbilisi. Can I do everything in one visit?

Many of our patients fly in from Israel and Europe. Send us your dates and what you have in mind through the booking form, and we will plan the consultation, the treatment and the follow-up check around your stay.
