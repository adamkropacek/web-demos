Body Filler Master Course

Advanced hands-on training in body contouring with fillers, for practitioners who already inject the face.

What you learn

Focus

Body filler indications, product selection, safe planes and volumes, cannula technique, complication prevention. Full syllabus on request.

How we teach

  • Theory in the morning, practice on live patients in the afternoon
  • Small groups, individual feedback from the instructor
  • Complication prevention and management in every module
  • Certificate on completion, support after the course
