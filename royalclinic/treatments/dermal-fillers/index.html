Dermal fillers

Lips, cheeks, chin, jawline, nose and under-eye hollows, shaped with hyaluronic acid.

About dermal fillers

Hyaluronic-acid fillers restore volume where the face has lost it and define what nature left soft: lips, cheeks, chin, jawline, nasolabial folds, tear troughs and the nose (non-surgical rhinoplasty). The effect is immediate and adjustable.

We work with needle or cannula depending on the area, choosing the safer technique for delicate zones such as the tear trough and the jawline. Products are original and certified, and you can see the documentation at your visit.

Good for

  • Thin or asymmetric lips
  • Flat cheeks, loss of volume
  • Weak chin or jawline
  • Nose bump or drooping tip
  • Nasolabial folds
  • Hollows under the eyes
Session
30 to 45 minutes
Downtime
Mild swelling for 1 to 3 days
First results
Immediately, settled after 2 weeks
Lasts
6 to 18 months, by area

The procedure

What happens at your visit

  1. Consultation and plan

    We agree the areas, the amount of product and the price before anything is done.

  2. Numbing and injection

    Numbing cream, then precise placement with needle or cannula. Usually under 45 minutes.

  3. Follow-up

    A check after two weeks, when swelling has settled, to confirm the result.

Questions

Dermal fillers questions

How long do fillers last?

Depending on the area and the product, typically 6 to 18 months. Lips tend to be shorter, cheeks and chin longer.

Can it be reversed?

Hyaluronic-acid fillers can be dissolved with an enzyme if ever needed, which is one reason we use them.

Will it look natural?

That is the goal. We add less rather than more and refine at the follow-up if you want a touch more.
