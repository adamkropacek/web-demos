// 01 - the problem

The problem is not too little information. It is too much - and every source is a silo with one narrative.

At a major event you cannot tell what actually happened, how solid the evidence is, what each side leaves out, or how it connects to the past - not without hours of your own OSINT. So you swallow one camp, or you drown in the noise. HORUS does neither for you. It shows you the shape, and you think.

// 02 - what HORUS does

It connects the dots. It does not judge.

01

Catch the debate gaining traction

Anywhere it surfaces - wires, OSINT, even a thread of comments. Relevance comes from traction over time, not from who is speaking. An anonymous claim starts at zero weight; weight grows as people, journalists and articles pile onto it.

02

Pull the rival claims

Side A throws X, side B throws Y. We extract the competing assertions instead of picking a winner.

03

Attach the formal material

Articles, reports, filings, satellite, theories - matched to each claim so you see what stands behind it.

04

Order by the KIND of evidence

A court record or a satellite reads differently from a comment - so it sits higher. That is visual prominence, descriptive of the evidence type. It is never a score of truth.

05

The strongest evidence always carries its opposition

Next to the satellite hangs the voice that disputes the satellite - even the fringe one. Nothing is hidden. You weigh it.

06

Show how it moved through time

How attention built or collapsed, who piled on, what got memory-holed. Then you connect the last dots and decide.

// 03 - the discipline

The whole product is what HORUS refuses to do.

NO

No verdict

The words "true" and "false" never leave the engine as a ruling. It shows the shape; you are the judge.

NO

No gatekeeping at the door

No source is filtered out by its attributes. Anyone may enter. Traction decides relevance, not a hidden bouncer.

NO

Volume moves nothing

A flood of mentions does not make a claim more credible or less. We show the shape of attention as data, and stop there.

NO

No arbiter, no "fake news"

That category is the arbiter's trap. Our enemy is the single-view silo, not a list of who is allowed to be right.
