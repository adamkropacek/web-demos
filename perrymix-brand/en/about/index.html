What we do

Manufacturing and service under one roof

01

What we do

Under the PERRYmix brand we manufacture new process equipment - HV-series vertical homogenizers, "Z" type kneaders, dissolvers and bead, basket and colloid mills. Our customers are companies in the chemical, pharmaceutical, food and feed industries.

02

Machines made to order

We apply a principle of individual approach - the final machine design meets the client's specific requirements. We rely on quality workshop facilities in Hradec Kralove. We have already exported our machines to more than 8 countries in the EU and Asia.

03

The HV vertical homogenizer

Our most successful product is the HV50-HV3000 vertical homogenizer (conical screw mixer). Its simple construction of a conical vessel and an orbiting arm with a rotating mixing screw gives the homogenizer a high degree of homogenization, a self-discharging effect and gentle mixing.

04

Service and support

As the manufacturer we provide service, spare parts and upgrades. Our service base is in Hradec Kralove. We focus on customer satisfaction and long-term partnership.
