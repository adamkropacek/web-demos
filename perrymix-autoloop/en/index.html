Frequently asked questions

What customers ask most often

The most frequent questions from procurement, R&D and QA before launching an inquiry. Did not find your answer? Write to us.

What is the lead time for VH-Series?

Standard 8-14 weeks from spec confirmation. Depends on size, ATEX design and required certificates. The exact date is part of the binding quote.

Do you provide IQ/OQ validation for pharma production?

Yes. IQ/OQ validation protocols are prepared together with Hosokawa Micron B.V. PQ we recommend through an independent validator. We deliver FAT, SAT and 3.1 material certificates as standard.

What is your service response time?

Service standby CZ/SK is 48 hours from report. Spare-parts stock in Hradec Králové. For strategic customers we offer extended 24-hour SLA under a service contract.

What about spare parts in the long term?

Hosokawa Micron guarantees spare-parts availability for at least 15 years from delivery. Critical parts (seals, rotor-stator heads) we hold in CZ stock. All original OEM, full manufacturer warranty.

Can the machine be financed by leasing or investment loan?

Yes. We cooperate with several financial partners (operating leasing, financial leasing, investment loan). Specific terms we pre-arrange together with the quote.

What if we need ATEX for Zone 0 / 20?

Standard is ATEX Zone 1 / 21 (zones with occasional explosive atmosphere). Zone 0 / 20 requires individual specification - please contact us for a technical consultation.
